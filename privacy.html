Privacy Policy

Last updated 11 November 2026

GigqPostiz is a private, self-hosted publishing tool that GigQ uses to post its own videos to the social media accounts it owns. This policy explains what information the tool handles and what happens to it.

Information we receive

When an account owner connects a TikTok account, TikTok shares the information covered by the permissions granted: the account's display name, username, profile picture, and basic profile statistics (follower, following, like and video counts); the list of videos the account has published and their statistics; and access and refresh tokens that let the tool publish on the account's behalf. Connecting YouTube, Instagram or Facebook accounts works the same way through those platforms' own sign-in.

The tool also stores the videos, captions and post settings the owner creates in it.

How we use it

This information is used only to show which account is connected, to publish videos the owner has created and scheduled, and to show the owner how those videos performed. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share this information with anyone. It is sent only to the platform it came from (for example, a video is uploaded to TikTok when the owner publishes it).

Storage and security

Everything is stored on GigQ's own server, which is reachable only over a private network. Access tokens are kept in the tool's database and are never exposed publicly.

Retention and deletion

Information is kept while an account stays connected. Removing an account from GigqPostiz stops all use of its tokens. You can also revoke access at any time in TikTok's settings, under Security and permissions, in the list of apps with access to your account; revoked tokens stop working immediately. To ask for any remaining data to be deleted, email the address below and it will be removed within 30 days.

Children

GigqPostiz is not directed at children and is not offered to the public.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
