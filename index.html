GigqPostiz

A private publishing tool for GigQ's own channels.

GigqPostiz is a privately run, self-hosted instance of Postiz, the open-source social media scheduler. GigQ uses it to schedule and publish its own original short videos to the TikTok, YouTube, Instagram and Facebook accounts it owns, such as Odd Clippings.

How it works with TikTok

The account owner connects a TikTok account with Login Kit and grants permission to read basic profile information and to publish videos. When a video is ready, the owner opens the post editor, picks the TikTok account, writes the caption, and chooses the privacy level, the comment, duet and stitch settings, and any commercial content disclosure. The video is then published to that account through TikTok's Content Posting API, either immediately or at the scheduled time. Post statistics are read back so the owner can see how each video performed.

Who can use it

GigqPostiz is not a public service. There is no sign-up. It runs on GigQ's own server and is used only by GigQ to manage GigQ's own accounts.
